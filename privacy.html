RIP CITY APP BETA

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Rip City App beta (the "App"), currently maintained by Derek Paris for authorized use by Rip City Academy. Rip City Academy determines facility membership and how its authorized staff use the App.

Information collected

Depending on how you use the App, information may include:

  • Name, email address, username, account status, and profile details.
  • Facility, program, training group, sport, position, and graduation information.
  • Workout assignments, exercise logs, attendance, habits, goals, and check-ins.
  • Athletic testing and performance results entered by authorized staff.
  • Announcements and related dismissal activity.
  • Authentication, security, and basic technical information needed to run the App.

How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Create, approve, secure, and support accounts.
  • Connect members with the correct facility, program, and training groups.
  • Deliver workouts, goals, habit tracking, announcements, and testing features.
  • Help authorized coaches and administrators support members and operate programs.
  • Maintain records, troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and improve the App.

Who may access information

Information may be available to the member, authorized Rip City Academy coaches and administrators, and the people maintaining the App when access is needed to operate or secure it. Service providers, including Supabase and Netlify, process information needed to provide database, authentication, storage, and hosting services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

The App is designed so one facility cannot access another facility's private information.

No sale or targeted advertising

Personal information is not sold and is not used for targeted advertising. If that practice changes, this Policy and any required choices will be updated before the change.

Retention, correction, and deletion

Information is retained while reasonably needed for active accounts, facility operations, security, backups, legal obligations, and historical training records. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact Rip City Academy or email parisathletesystems@gmail.com. Some records may be retained when legally permitted or required.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No online system can guarantee perfect security. Report suspected unauthorized account access promptly to Rip City Academy and the contact address below.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and users must confirm they are at least 13 before creating an account. Users under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian. If information from a child under 13 is discovered, the account will be disabled and the information will be reviewed for deletion.

Policy changes and contact

This Policy may be updated as the beta and its business structure develop. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date and, when appropriate, a new acknowledgment. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to parisathletesystems@gmail.com.
