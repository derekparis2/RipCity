# Rip City Beta Test Checklist

Use this checklist when walking Rip City testers through the app.

## Before Testing

- Make sure the tester has the correct role: coach, athlete, or H2K member.
- Make sure their account is approved by a coach/admin.
- Ask them to use the app on the device they would normally use.
- Ask them to submit feedback using the Feedback link in the app.

## Coach Tests

- Log in as a coach/admin.
- Review pending signups and approve a test member.
- Open Roster.
- Confirm members appear in the right program type: Athlete or H2K.
- Confirm member profile pictures appear in roster cards when uploaded.
- Add or change a member's groups.
- Change an H2K member's band color.
- Create a workout from the Workout Builder.
- Use the exercise library search while building a workout.
- Assign a workout to one group.
- Assign a workout to multiple groups.
- Reuse or reassign an existing workout.
- Open Coach Dashboard and review workout completion.
- Submit one coach feedback form response from a coach page.

## H2K Member Tests

- Log in as an H2K member.
- Confirm the dashboard shows H2K habit score cards.
- Complete and un-complete daily habits.
- Refresh the page and confirm habit progress stays saved.
- Open today's workout if one is assigned.
- Save one set.
- Save all sets.
- Refresh the workout and confirm saved set results reload.
- Open Future Workouts and click through months.
- Open Past Workouts and review a previous workout.
- Upload or change a profile picture.
- Confirm the profile picture appears on the dashboard.
- Confirm the profile picture appears to coaches on roster/dashboard cards.

## Athlete Tests

- Log in as an athlete.
- Confirm H2K habits do not show.
- Confirm athlete workout stats show.
- Open today's assigned workout if one exists.
- Save one set.
- Save all sets.
- Refresh the workout and confirm saved set results reload.
- Open Future Workouts and Past Workouts.
- Upload or change a profile picture.
- Confirm the profile picture appears on the dashboard.

## Feedback Questions

- What felt confusing?
- What felt slower than expected?
- What did you expect to be able to click but could not?
- Did anything save incorrectly or disappear after refresh?
- Was the workout builder easy enough for coaches?
- Was logging workout results easy enough on a phone?
- What should be fixed before more people use it?

## Known Unfinished Areas

- Goals and progress charts are not finished.
- Leaderboards are not finished.
- Coach notes are not finished.
- Parent access is not enabled yet.
- Calculated maxes and PR tracking need more product decisions.
